STORY TIME
THE MOONLIGHT ADVENTURES · PART 1

Oliver and the
sleepy forest

6 min read · Oliver & Luna

Just as the last light slipped behind the garden gate, Oliver noticed something rather unusual. The little path beyond the apple tree was glowing.

Not a bright, wake-you-up sort of glow. A soft, honey-coloured glow, like a night-light left on by someone who knew you were coming.

“Luna,” he whispered, pulling on his yellow coat. “I think the forest is getting ready for bed.”

A very small invitation

Luna lifted one sleepy ear. Beside the gate lay a leaf, folded neatly into an envelope. Inside were just four words:

Will you help the moon?

Oliver took Luna’s paw. Together, they followed the gentle light between the trees, where a hundred little lanterns swayed in the evening breeze.

✦ A little pause to imagine ✦

And when every lantern was glowing, Oliver and Luna curled up beneath the oldest tree. Above them, the moon smiled. There would be more adventures tomorrow. For tonight, the forest could rest.
