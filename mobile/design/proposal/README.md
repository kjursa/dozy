# Settings and story credits proposal

Open `index.html` and follow the links to review the proposal screens.

## Proposed flow

- Home has a small Settings button in the top toolbar and a compact story-credit balance card below the primary story action.
- Settings puts the remaining credit balance first and uses a gentle Get Credits button to open the bundle screen, followed by account preferences. Use the review controls to switch between the default four-credit balance and the empty-balance state.
- The credit-pack screen lets the user choose one bundle and continue to a purchase review step.
- The purchase step repeats the selected quantity and one-time price before handing off to the platform purchase sheet.
- Create Story shows a contextual empty-balance message only when the user cannot generate a story, with a direct link to the packs.

The Home card is a gentle reminder, Settings is the persistent place to check the balance and reach bundles, and the Create Story message handles the blocking case. Credits are bought as one-time bundles; there is no subscription or recurring renewal.

All account details and bundle prices are mock content. The pack prices are placeholders for product discussion, and the store handoff is simulated; no purchase is made.
